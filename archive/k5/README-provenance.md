- Single rail track only; no Gustav-style paired railcars or dual-track broad carriage
- Long slender near-horizontal barrel, low support cheeks, deep broad-slab center body, small tail platform
- Top view reads as straight-down orthographic plan with no visible vertical side walls or converging lines
- Corrected front view has no receding railway, but AI shading still suggests small depth around muzzle/support; treat as art guidance
- Overall silhouette and two bogies agree across views; external sleeve step spacing/diameters vary slightly between side and top. Do not trace these as exact geometric correspondence
- Hidden surfaces, top deck, small fittings, davit and platform placement are artistic inferences
- Detail panel 02 has exactly six visible wheels; panel 03 is a cropped perspective material/form view, not an axle-count reference
- No dimensions, internal mechanisms, ballistic information, weapon operation or manufacturing data provided. Model must remain nonfunctional decorative geometry
