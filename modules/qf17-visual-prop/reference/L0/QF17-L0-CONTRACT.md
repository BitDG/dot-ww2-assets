# QF17 L0: concept and identity checkpoint only

This checkpoint is reference-stage work, not a completed 3D model.

Identity: QF 17-pr Mk I on Carriage 17-pr Mk I, as named in Nationaal Militair Museum object 00057312 (1943 catalogue title). Source: https://collectie.nmm.nl/nl/collectie/detail/847144/ . The RCA April 2022 newsletter is usage context only and does not identify a carriage Mk.

Fixed intended pose: two wheels grounded, horizontal barrel, twin trails spread for static display. Standard separate 17-pr carriage; not the temporary 25-pr Pheasant mounting or tank gun. The asset is unmeasured, nonfunctional game appearance. No internal weapon parts, manufacturing measurements, ammunition, fire control, ballistics or real operation. Muzzle is uncovered with a shallow closed artistic face. Hidden faces and small fittings are artistic interpretation.

Frozen concepts: QF17-three-view-v3.png and QF17-six-details-v2.png. Both follow the supplied Gustav layout: independent three-view sheet plus an independent 3-column, 2-row exterior-detail sheet. Revised to remove circular jack feet in favor of angular tail-spade silhouettes and improve front orthographic presentation. These are AI-generated concepts, not Blender renders or survey drawings.

Planned PGF parts: barrel exterior, shield, carriage exterior, left/right wheel pivots, left/right trail exteriors, cosmetic fittings. Three detail tiers; bounded artScale 0.65–1.5; paint, roughness, weathering and PGF camouflage; deterministic seed affects cosmetic appearance. These planned capabilities are not certified at L0.

Reuse: wheel-assembly tire geometry, material-kit metalFinishPixels, portable vehicle camouflage, shared AssetIR/GLB export and resource ownership. No independent material baker.

Observation-only museum images are excluded from this checkpoint and all output assets; no museum photo was provided to image generation, used as texture or bundled for publication.
